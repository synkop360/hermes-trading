# Small-account fixed-expiry feasibility

September 29, 2026: no candidate qualifies from the current quote snapshot. This extends the earlier BTC/ETH spot-carry inspection to small SOL contracts and derivatives-only hedges. It is not a repeat backtest or evidence that all future opportunities are impossible.

Public instruments and ticker responses, with source URLs, UTC retrieval times and SHA256 hashes, are saved in `research/runs/dated-small-account-v1/`. Ticker server time: 2026-09-29 06:22:43 UTC. All ten listed linear fixed-expiry contracts were screened. Public metadata is not authenticated French-account eligibility; quotes are not atomic fills.

Official EEA specifications list dated BTC, ETH and SOL contracts. SOL has monthly and quarterly maturities with a 0.01 SOL minimum. ETH's 0.001 minimum requires approximately USD2.69–2.76 at snapshot asks; BTC's 0.0001 requires USD8.39–8.60. Both exceed the USD2.50 per-symbol budget. SOL's minimum is about USD1.19. One 0.01 SOL dated/perpetual pair has approximately USD2.38–2.39 combined opening notional, fitting even a conservative USD2.50 aggregate-underlying cap before buffers. Doubling both quantities would exceed that cap. Source: [Kraken EEA specifications](https://support.kraken.com/articles/perpetual-contract-specifications-for-clients-in-the-eea).

Displayed sizes cover one minimum-sized pair, but that does not guarantee execution or later exit liquidity. The October bid shows only 0.01 SOL. Volume and quotes are a single snapshot, not a historical liquidity assessment.

Selling dated and buying perpetual gives an entry-price difference of **-USD0.0075** for October or **-USD0.0116** for December at 0.01 SOL. Buying dated and selling perpetual gives **-USD0.0022** or **-USD0.0054**. All four are negative before entry fees of about USD0.00119, eventual exit/settlement fees, funding and other costs. These differences are components of hypothetical convergence P&L, not cash proceeds credited on futures entry, and not complete position returns.

The lower entry gap on long-dated/short-perpetual could be offset by future funding receipts, but funding is variable, can reverse sign, and cannot be locked from a current displayed rate. It remains a financing-risk hypothesis related to previously tested carry. At dated settlement the perpetual must also be closed; its execution price need not equal the dated contract's index average. Hence this is not a guaranteed convergence trade.

Using two dated SOL contracts avoids perpetual funding but introduces a calendar-spread risk. Buying October and selling December costs USD0.0137 in entry-price difference at minimum quantity; the reverse costs USD0.0128. The contracts mature on different dates, so the spread is not forced to zero at a common settlement. Holding one beyond the other's maturity leaves directional exposure. A higher quoted far-month price alone would not establish arbitrage either.

Kraken's dated settlement uses an index average over 07:30–08:00 UTC, with a taker settlement fee. EEA initial margin starts at 10%, maintenance at half initial; actual eligibility and account conditions require verification. Do not use the more permissive generic margin values in a public response as the French-account rules. Gross exposure caps remain unchanged; no leverage increase, spot borrowing, transfer, paid service or order has been initiated. Source: [contract and settlement rules](https://support.kraken.com/articles/perpetual-contract-specifications-for-clients-in-the-eea).

Decision: **do not start a fixed-expiry strategy from this evidence**. A financing-sensitive or calendar-spread candidate would require a distinct frozen hypothesis, historical executable spread data and funding/collateral accounting, not just candle prices or an extrapolation of today's funding. If usable public bid/ask histories are unavailable, collect prospective public observations before designing a backtest that assumes fills inside these wide spreads. No profitability claim is supported.

The first five samples contain one null-sided daily record each; September2026 contains two. All other returned daily quotes are positive and uncrossed. This is only daily sample validation: it does not cure the hourly missing-side problem, establish intraday exit liquidity, or establish any strategy returns. Missing end-of-life quotes cannot be replaced by a synthetic executable price; settlement requires its actual index rule and fee.

Raw instrument inventory, chart-market list, six responses, URL/time/hash manifests, contract metadata and quality/null-date summaries are saved under `expired-discovery/`. No contract was selected by trading profit and no strategy was fitted. This evidence changes the next action: historical analysis is potentially feasible across multiple expiries, rather than restricted to the active contract's short history. Before a full backtest, register the economic hypothesis and chronological design, then collect and validate hourly paired quotes, funding where applicable, settlement references and conservative position/execution accounting. A longer archive does not by itself establish an edge. Independent review remains pending.
