## Return to the intended product

1. **Make the deployed paper loop trustworthy.** Identify which code/config actually runs and which process owns strategy changes. Diagnose reflection failures. Stage verified local fixes as a concrete patch before any deployment approval. Keep one writer and an explicit model/version, cadence and failure policy. Do not claim the supervisor survives a sleeping local machine unless that behavior is demonstrated.
2. **Restore one variable per experiment.** Give each hypothesis one candidate version and one named variable; retain a fixed control on the same contemporaneous data. If evaluating multiple assets, apply one global change or stagger one asset experiment at a time. Keep five-trade reviews for diagnosis/proposals; require sufficient subsequent evidence before claiming that the change improved profitability. Allow a logged no-change decision.
3. **Measure the actual wallet.** Score rolling30-day net liquidation equity, drawdown and daily-return risk quality, including open positions, spreads, fees, funding where applicable, model/tool costs and hosting. Record missing costs explicitly. Use EUR50/100/150/200 scenarios and verified tradable markets. Reconcile goals and safeguards into one effective policy before launch; do not loosen risk limits merely to chase the return target.
4. **Run a bounded adaptive paper experiment after operational preflight.** The October8 fixed protocol is useful as a control, not the complete product. Before starting, finalize the exact LLM action menu, cost budget, candidate/control accounting, review cadence, minimum evidence and stopping rules. A30-day operational/learning checkpoint can show reliability, costs, opportunities and provisional outcomes; it cannot certify a winning strategy. Retain the longer evaluation needed for performance qualification without making it a condition for gathering safe paper evidence.
5. **Focus research on one blocker or candidate at a time.** Put broad strategy hunting and new data infrastructure behind explicit questions. Reuse the completed rejection evidence. First test the adaptive loop's incremental value on a feasible candidate; open another research family only when evidence rejects it or data/market access makes it infeasible. No strategy is guaranteed to win, and live orders remain a separate decision after qualification.

Immediate next work should be the reflection/runtime/account-metric reconciliation, rather than expanding the perpetual collector while the existing worker continues with mismatched markets, costs and code. Preserve completed research and journal primitives for later use. No deletion or rollback was performed by this review.
