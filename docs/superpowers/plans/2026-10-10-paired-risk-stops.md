# Persistent paired paper drawdown stops

Implement the approved3%net-equity-peak policy from paired-paper-runner.md. Each virtual account starts with its fixed initial capital peak; measured net liquidation equity updates the peak. Unknown equity cannot advance it or erase a stop. A breach in either nonprimary arm halts both arms at that wallet size. Either EUR100primary arm breaching halts all accounts and steering. Halts never clear automatically after equity recovery or restart.

Persist risk state in the primary publication database with batch/account checkpoints. Check frozen pre-batch budgets before any account trades and post-trade valuations after account outcomes. Liquidate halted simulated inventory at the captured bid with original entry-version fees/slippage, using a separately identified risk event so an already processed strategy candle does not suppress the risk exit. Halted accounts still observe ongoing costs. Unknown/missing quotes leave liquidation pending rather than inventing fills. Risk-close records are operational outcomes, not learning evidence. Supervisor snapshot/publication refuses a primary halt.

Separate trading from final observation: compute post-trade values for risk decisions, complete necessary risk liquidations, then journal one final wallet observation per batch timestamp. Preserve logical captured time and actual recovery recording time. Replay checkpoints remain idempotent across risk events and final reports. Add tests for exact3%boundary, per-pair/global stop, monotone peak/latched recovery, unknown costs, continuing expenses, publication/review block and partial-commit restart. Full verification and independent review before any activation. No paid model, service, backtest, deployment or real order.
