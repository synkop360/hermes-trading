## Fixed rotation protocol

At each first-of-month UTC daily open, use only prior completed daily bars. For each of BTC/USDT and ETH/USDT calculate prior close / close 90 calendar days earlier - 1. Require 91 contiguous completed daily bars. Select the greatest strictly positive return, ties BTC; otherwise cash. Keep an unchanged selection without trading or resetting its entry. A change liquidates the prior asset and purchases the new one at that day's open. No leverage, shorting, adding, stop, or intra-month signal. This is a new low-turnover candidate, not a retest of the rejected breakout.

Primary cost assumption: 0.8% commission plus 0.1% slippage per leg; stress: 0.8% fee plus 0.3% slippage. Exact multiplicative cash accounting. End liquidation at last close; mark daily equity at estimated liquidation proceeds. Research sleeve uses 100% of its virtual cash and is NOT a proposal to allocate the real account at that exposure. No account minimums or hosting bills modeled at this screening stage. Any advancement requires actual bot-risk sizing, minimums and operating-cost feasibility before paper readiness.

Use already checksum-verified daily Binance archives Jan 2018-Aug 2026; 2018 warmup. Chronological partitions: development 2019-2022; evaluation 2023-2024; final retrospective check Jan2025-Aug2026. Each starts fresh at 10,000 virtual units for normalized returns. These periods are previously inspected project data, NOT unseen validation. Do not select or change parameters using any result. Benchmark fresh BTC buy-and-hold and an initial 50/50 BTC/ETH buy-and-hold allocation, identical fees/slippage and dates, no rebalancing.

Screen rejects if either evaluation's net return <=0, stress return <=0, or daily max drawdown >30%. A positive screen is only provisional: report annual returns, completed holdings, win rate, drawdowns, market exposure, and benchmarks. Both evaluations must have at least 12 active months and 6 completed holdings; otherwise evidence is sparse. Do not label this winning from a positive total alone. Further work on a passing candidate must assess sample uncertainty and benchmark-relative evidence, Kraken price portability, actual risk/minimum/cost constraints, and future paper observations. No parameter optimization in this experiment.
