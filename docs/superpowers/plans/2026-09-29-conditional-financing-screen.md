# Conditional dated/perpetual financing screen

Registered September29 before event outcomes. Research-only; USD50, France, existing risk limits, no orders or paid services. This is a related carry-family trial, not independent new evidence. Previous failed spot/perpetual carry remains in the ledger. Economic hypothesis: persistent perpetual financing demand may sometimes pay more than the dated-contract premium and trading costs. Positive future funding is not guaranteed; this is not locked arbitrage.

Universe: SOL/USD linear perpetual and the nearest-expiry listed SOL linear dated contract with at least9 and at most35 calendar days until expiry at decision. Choose nearest expiry, ties symbol order; do not search contracts for best quoted profit. Use recorded opening/expiry timestamps, including expired inventory. Fixed position: long0.01 SOL dated and short0.01 SOL perpetual. Gross underlying exposure must be <=5% of current account equity, total<=10%; do not net away the hedge for caps. No leverage multiplier, adding or parameter sweep.

Schedule: Mondays00:00 UTC, October6,2025 through August31,2026. Use quote bucket Sunday23:00 only after its close at Monday00:00. Use168 funding rates from decision-168h through decision-1h, requiring all present, finite. Positive means funding received by a short. No current incomplete hour. Enter using the next completed hourly quote bucket labelled Monday01:00, economically timestamp Monday02:00. This delays two hours from signal. Exit168h after entry, with corresponding completed bucket, before expiry. Suppress overlapping entries (decision before last exit); do not exploit future entry quotes for signal selection. Missing entry quotes cancel the whole paired entry; no legging assumed successful. Preserve cancellation counts and acknowledge this atomic-entry idealization.

Selection gate per coin: trailing168h absolute funding sum must be positive and greater than max(0,dated_ask-perp_bid) +2*(fee+slippage)*(dated_ask+perp_bid), from decision quotes. Fee0.0005 and adverse slippage0.001 per fill; stress slippage0.003 on the same selected events, no re-selection. The forecast assumes last week's receipts repeat and conservatively covers the full positive entry premium, not merely its amortized fraction. This rule is a fixed economic screening choice, not a calibrated predictor. The dated/perpetual basis may widen; no expectation of zero exit spread is used in realized accounting.

Execution: use adverse bid/ask fills, exact quantities and fees on each actual entry/exit notional. Futures sale does not supply synthetic cash principal. Starting equityUSD50, P&L additive in USD. Mark both legs to liquidation-side quotes each completed hour, include exit-fee reserve. Funding labelled hour h applies to [h,h+1), supported by live/history match September29 at16:27 and official rate-setting examples; include exactly entry<=h<exit. Positive rate credits the short; negative debits. Funding gaps cannot become zero. If a held interval or liquidation quote is unavailable, label that event unscorable and the account path incomplete; do not present the surviving subset as full performance. Conservative stress diagnostic may carry an unknown-event flag but cannot repair it.

Risk path: gross cap includes both legs; .01 positions only. Entry must fit using stressed entry notionals and fee buffer. No entry if risk sizing cannot satisfy existing per-trade budget. For initial falsification, distinguish unit-event research from executable-account results; only the latter can support advancement. Enforce account3% drawdown and0.5% daily-loss stops using next available adverse quotes, document gaps and stop slippage. Exact stop sizing and liquidation logic must be specified/tested before claiming account validity. No existing live risk setting changes.

First stage reports data availability, causal candidate counts and entry feasibility only, without P&L. If sufficient, implement and test event accounting before outcomes. Require at least20 non-overlapping scorable holds per evaluation and no incomplete held paths to advance to statistical validation.
