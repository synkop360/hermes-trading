# SOL/XRP pair relationship prerequisite

September29,2026. **Do not advance this fixed pair to a mean-reversion backtest on the basis of this diagnostic.** This is a statistical prerequisite, not a profitability test and not a rejection of every possible pairs strategy.

Using 365 aligned daily closes from calendar2022, the fixed log(SOL)-on-log(XRP) regression does not provide evidence of cointegration at the registered5% threshold. Engle-Granger statistic is -0.7351, p=0.9427, versus the5% critical value -3.3530. A high p-value is a failure to reject no cointegration, not a probability that the null is true or proof that mean reversion can never occur.

Daily log-return correlation is0.7255. This shared directional movement does not imply a stable tradable price relationship. The full-year hedge coefficient is1.7649, compared with1.3325 in the first half and0.3284 in the second half. These half-year fits are descriptive diagnostics, not competing strategy settings. The fitted residual AR(1) coefficient is0.99567; its conditional159.9-day half-life must not be treated as a reliable holding period because stationarity was not established.

The input-series unit-root diagnostics are compatible with the test's integration assumption: level ADF p-values SOL0.9179/XRP0.6250; first-difference p-values below1.8e-10. Those tests do not prove the assumption. Fixed7-lag daily tests use a constant and no automatic lag selection. Only2022 was scored; later strategy returns were not calculated. This pair was shortlisted for small order sizes, not an independently established economic linkage. No search over other pairs, regression direction, window lengths or lag settings was performed.

At the published Kraken base derivatives taker rate0.05%, entering and exiting two equal-dollar legs costs approximately0.10% of gross opening notional if prices are unchanged. Our assumed0.10% adverse execution per fill adds approximately0.20%, for a0.30% initial hurdle before funding, price-dependent exit fees and operating costs. On USD5 gross exposure this is about USD0.015, while every USD1 monthly bill consumes2% of the USD50 account. These are illustrative arithmetic, not measured fills or expected returns. [Kraken EEA fees](https://support.kraken.com/articles/fees-for-derivatives-trading-eea).

Research motivation came from [Tadi and Kortchemski](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.10662), whose study considers cointegration and bid/ask execution in other cryptocurrency contracts. Our daily fixed-pair diagnostic is not a replication and does not inherit that paper's results. The application of [Engle-Granger](https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/generated/statsmodels.tsa.stattools.coint.html) follows its no-cointegration null and fixed-lag option.
